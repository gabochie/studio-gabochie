Frequently Asked Questions

If you're already registered, log in to your Membership Dashboard and click Upgrade on your chosen tier. You'll be guided through a secure payment via Flutterwave (card, mobile money, or USSD). Your new tier activates immediately.
There is no contract. You can cancel your subscription at any time from your dashboard. Premium and VIP are billed monthly. Downgrading takes effect at the end of your current billing period — you keep access until then.
We accept credit/debit cards, mobile money (MTN, Vodafone, AirtelTigo Ghana), and USSD. All payments are processed securely by Flutterwave. Prices are in Ghana Cedis. International supporters can use cards — your bank handles the conversion.
Yes. Upgrading mid-month is prorated — you get instant VIP access and are billed the difference. Downgrading from VIP to a lower tier takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle.
When a paid membership expires, your account reverts to Free tier. You will lose access to Premium/VIP content, downloads, and mentorship. Your public content access (courses you browsed, articles, newsletter) remains. You can re-subscribe at any time.
We believe formation should be accessible. If the price of Premium or VIP is genuinely out of reach, email us at info@gideonabochie.com — we review requests on a case-by-case basis and may offer a reduced rate or sponsored access.
