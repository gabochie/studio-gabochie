Frequently Asked Questions

If you're already registered, log in to your Membership Dashboard and click Upgrade on your chosen tier. You'll be guided through a secure payment via Flutterwave (card, mobile money, or USSD). Your new tier activates immediately.
There is no contract. You can cancel your subscription at any time from your dashboard. Supporter, Scholar, and Patron are billed monthly or yearly depending on your selection. Founding Partner is annual only. Downgrading takes effect at the end of your current billing period — you keep access until then.
We accept credit/debit cards, mobile money (MTN, Vodafone, AirtelTigo Ghana), and USSD. All payments are processed securely by Flutterwave. Prices are in Ghana Cedis. International supporters can use cards — your bank handles the conversion.
Yes. Upgrading mid-cycle is prorated — you get instant access and are billed the difference. Downgrading takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle. Founding Partner is a standalone annual tier and can be held alongside any other plan.
When a paid membership expires, your account reverts to Free tier. You will lose access to paid-tier content, downloads, and mentorship. Your public content access (courses you browsed, articles, newsletter) remains. You can re-subscribe at any time.
We believe formation should be accessible. If the price of Scholar or Patron is genuinely out of reach, email us at info@gideonabochie.com — we review requests on a case-by-case basis and may offer a reduced rate or sponsored access.
