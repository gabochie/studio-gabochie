Back Matter GLOSSARY OF TERMS THE DIVINE ALGORITHM
"Know your terms — the wise are never lost for words!"

"My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge." — Hosea 4:6. This glossary equips you with the language of the AI age, interpreted through a Kingdom lens.

ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE (AI)
Computer systems trained on human-generated data to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as language, reasoning, and pattern recognition. Operates within God-given limits of creation.
LARGE LANGUAGE MODEL (LLM)
A type of AI trained on vast amounts of text to understand and generate human language. Examples: Claude, GPT-4. A powerful tool for Kingdom content creation when used with discernment.
GENERATIVE AI
AI systems that create original content — text, images, music, video, code. The creative expression of human-taught patterns; not a replacement for Spirit-inspired creativity.
NEURAL NETWORK
A computational architecture loosely modeled on the human brain, consisting of interconnected nodes that process information. A reflection of the complexity God built into human cognition.
PROMPT ENGINEERING
The craft of communicating effectively with AI systems to receive high-quality, targeted outputs. The modern equivalent of asking the right question at the right time.
MACHINE LEARNING
A branch of AI in which systems improve their performance through exposure to data rather than explicit programming. Systems trained, not created — unlike the Holy Spirit, Who creates.
DEEPFAKE
AI-generated audio or video content that realistically depicts real people saying or doing things they did not. A significant deception risk requiring biblical discernment and verification disciplines.
KINGDOM OF GOD
The reign and rule of God expressed through the person of Jesus Christ, in the hearts of believers, and progressively through history toward the fullness of Christ's return. Not geographical; transformational.
THEOLOGY
The systematic study of the nature of God, divine revelation, and the relationship between God and humanity. AI can assist theology; only the Holy Spirit can reveal God to the spirit of a person.
DISCERNMENT (SPIRITUAL)
The Spirit-given ability to distinguish truth from error, the genuine from the counterfeit, and the divine from the demonic. The primary weapon of the believer in the information age.
DIGITAL DISCIPLESHIP
The intentional formation of Christian character, doctrine, and spiritual practice through digital platforms and tools. Must be rooted in the local church body, not replace it.
KINGDOM ECONOMICS
A biblical framework for building wealth, enterprise, and financial systems that are governed by Kingdom values — generosity, justice, sustainability, and mission funding.
ANOINTING
The tangible presence and empowerment of the Holy Spirit upon a person for a specific purpose or work. Cannot be replicated, automated, or synthesized by technology.
LUMINOS ✦
The star character guide in this report — a symbolic representation of divine wisdom (luminosity) and the Holy Spirit's illuminating work, helping believers navigate the intersection of faith and the AI age.
