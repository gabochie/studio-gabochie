Introduction — The Fear That Came at Night

I know what it is to be afraid at night.

The house is quiet. Everybody is sleeping. And your mind starts to work. Who is doing this to me? Why is it always my family? Why does every good thing die just before it arrives?

For years I carried that fear like a stone in my chest. I kept no charm in my pocket — my "just in case" was running from the call, and I kept that flight secret, even from people who loved me. Whenever I stopped running, the whole day felt exposed.

I prayed. I fasted. I attended more all-nights than I can count — in Accra, in Kumasi, most of them in Accra. I stood in prayer lines. I was anointed with oil more times than I can remember. And still, some nights, I lay awake listening to my own heartbeat, sure that someone, somewhere, was working against me.

Then one Sunday, a preacher read a verse I had heard all my life but never really heard:

"Rebellion is as the sin of witchcraft." — 1 Samuel 15:23

He was not talking about my auntie. He was not talking about the woman in the village people whispered about. He was talking about me.

I had been looking for the witch outside my gate. God was pointing me to the one inside my own heart — my own stubborn refusal to obey what He had already told me to do: to step into full-time ministry, a call I kept postponing.

That day the fear did not leave because I found a stronger charm. It left because I finally told the truth about myself. I repented. I burned what I had been hiding. I filled the empty space in my life with God's Word, His Spirit, and His people. And the nights became quiet.

I am not writing this as a man who has arrived. I am writing as a man who has walked the road and knows the way out. The steps in this little book are the steps I took. They are simple on purpose. Read one step in the evening, do the action the same day, and speak the declaration for a week.

You do not need to be afraid of the dark any more. The One who made the night is stronger than anyone who works in it.

Turn the page. Let us take Step 1 together.

Step 1 — Rebellion Is Witchcraft

7 Fearless Steps From Witchcraft to Greater Ghanaian Destiny

"Rebellion is as the sin of witchcraft." — 1 Samuel 15:23

The woman in Kumasi

She waited after church. Tired eyes. Bible in a plastic bag.

"Pastor, they say my auntie is blocking my destiny. I have done three all-nights. When will I be free?"

I asked her one question:

"If God told you clearly what to do this week, would you do it — even if it costs you?"

Silence. That silence is where this book starts.

Most of us look for the witch outside. The Bible starts with the one inside.

Saul never visited a shrine

King Saul did not go to a juju house. He just refused to obey God fully.

God said: destroy everything in Amalek (1 Samuel 15:3).

Saul kept the best sheep "for sacrifice" (verse 15).

Then he blamed the people (verse 21).

Samuel's answer is our key verse:

Rebellion IS witchcraft.

Not "leads to." Not "opens the door to." It IS.

Saul did witchcraft on himself — by choosing his own way over God's way.

My point in one line: your destiny is first blocked not by somebody's juju, but by your own disobedience to what God already told you.

Outside witchcraft is real. But it feeds on this first one.

What does God say?

Short and clear:

1. Don't replace Him. "No divination, no sorcery, no omens, no spells, no mediums" (Deuteronomy 18:10-11). Why? "Anyone who does these is detestable to the LORD" (verse 12). It is not about power. It is about loyalty. Morning fasting + evening horoscope = God rejected.

2. It cannot beat Him. Pharaoh's magicians copied Moses until they broke and said, "This is the finger of God" (Exodus 8:19). Balaam was hired to curse Israel and found: "No spell works against Jacob" (Numbers 23:23).

3. He forgives those who turn. King Manasseh did witchcraft — then repented in prison, and God brought him back (2 Chronicles 33:12-13). In Ephesus, new believers burned their magic books openly (Acts 19:19). No stoning. Just fire and freedom.

One warning for Ghana: Exodus 22:18 was a law for old Israel's courts. It is never permission to beat, accuse, or drive out grandmothers and children. We fight demons and disobedience — never old women. This book stands against witch-camp abuse.

The 3 masks of Saul

1. Half obedience. "I obeyed... but I kept the best." The trader pays tithe but cheats at customs, then binds "poverty." God calls that divination — trying to control the outcome while keeping control.

2. Church cover. Saul said the sheep were "for sacrifice." All-nights and oil cannot cover a hard heart. "To obey is better than sacrifice" (1 Samuel 15:22).

3. Blame game. "The people did it." As long as you have an auntie to blame, you never face your own mirror.

Write this down: My first witch is me when I refuse God.

🙏 Pause & Pray — "Lord, show me my Saul-spot. I stop blaming. I listen." 📖 Psalm for this step — "Create in me a clean heart, O God." (Psalm 51:10)

Your Step 1 — 30 minutes today

Grab a notebook and a quiet corner.

1. Name it. Read 1 Samuel 15. Pray: "Lord, where am I Saul?" Write ONE clear disobedience. Not your enemies. Yours. Forgive who? Repay who? Leave what? Confess what?

2. Say it. Pray aloud: "Father, I call rebellion what You call it — witchcraft. I repent. Wash me in the blood of Jesus" (1 John 1:9).

3. Burn it. Delete the mallam's number. Unfollow the horoscope. Throw away the "protection." If you keep it "just in case," you kept the god.

4. Obey in 48 hours. Call. Pay. Go. Quit. Confess. Tell one trusted believer so they can ask you next week.

5. Fill the house. A swept house left empty invites worse spirits (Matthew 12:43-45). Start daily Word — Deuteronomy 18, Galatians 5 — and stay in a Bible church. The Holy Spirit is the Tenant who keeps the house free.

Pray this now

"LORD God, I come to You — not to the dead, not to fear. No spell stands against Your child. I turn from my rebellion. Wash me in Jesus' blood. Fill me with Your Spirit. I break agreement with every curse and covenant, by the cross. I receive my Greater Ghanaian Destiny in Christ. Amen."

Say this for 7 days

I am defined by Christ, not divined by stars. My obedience is my protection. No curse holds what the cross freed. Your Kingdom come through me. I will fulfill my Greater Ghanaian Destiny — for family, church, and nation.
