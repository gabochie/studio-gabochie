Lesson 4 — Your First Sound: E Minor

Every house has a hall — the room that fills with whatever is spoken in it. The first chord we build is E minor (Em): two fingers, and it welcomes all six strings. A day-one sound that fills the whole room.

Index (1st finger)2nd fret · A string (5th) — press with the fingertip, close behind the metal bar
Middle (2nd finger)2nd fret · D string (4th) — neck upright, claw hand, thumb low
StrumAll six strings downward, arm like a pendulum near the soundhole
E MINOR
The hall · two fingers · all six strings ring
Sound check — strum from the thickest string; all six ring clear.
Refuse — lying-flat fingers; thumb hooked over the top.
Checkpoint
  • Tune — six strings read E·A·D·G·B·e in order
  • Position — occupant's hold, 60 seconds, breathing normally
  • Five clean Em strums
  • One more strum — just to listen
