Gideon Abochie

Founder, GideonAbochie Studio — Systems Thinker, Educator, Creative

Gideon Abochie is a systems thinker, educator, and creative professional based in Ghana, West Africa. He founded GideonAbochie Studio to equip a generation of leaders with biblical truth, creative excellence, and the mental models needed to navigate an increasingly complex world.

His work spans curriculum design, writing, and teaching — with a focus on making Systems Thinking accessible through a biblical worldview. He is the author of Systems Thinking in Genesis and Architectural Thinking Vol. 1, among other works.

Gideon speaks on topics including Systems Thinking, Biblical Leadership, Creative Economy in Africa, and Faith & Work integration. He is available for podcasts, conferences, guest lectures, and media interviews.
