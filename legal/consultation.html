Booking & Session Terms

Last updated: October 2026 · Template — your confirmed booking and invoice reference these terms

These terms govern consultations booked through studio.gabochie.com/consultation — Anchor Sessions, Coaching Sprints and Advisory Retainers with Gideon Abochie.

1. Requesting a Slot

A booking is confirmed when you receive a written confirmation with a date, time and invoice. Requesting a slot does not create an obligation — no card is required to request, and you pay only after confirmation.

2. Payment

  • Sessions are invoiced on confirmation and paid before the session — mobile money or card in Ghana Cedis.
  • Coaching Sprints are invoiced for the 4-session block up front. Retainers are invoiced monthly in advance.
  • A session is not guaranteed until payment is received; if someone else requests your confirmed-and-unpaid slot first and pays, we will offer you the next available time.

3. Rescheduling & Cancellation

  • Reschedule freely up to 24 hours before the session — your invoice carries to the new slot at no charge.
  • Inside 24 hours, or a no-show, the session is forfeited. Sprint and retainer sessions may be rescheduled once at no charge as a courtesy.
  • If we cancel or move a session, you may take the new slot or receive a full refund of that session.
  • Sprint blocks: after session one, unused sessions may be refunded pro-rata on request. Once session two begins, the block is non-refundable but sessions never expire while the sprint is active.

4. What a Session Includes

  • The live session at the booked duration, online (or in-person in Accra where arranged).
  • Written session notes and next steps, sent within 24 hours.
  • For Anchor Sessions: one follow-up question answered by email.

Consultation is advice and thinking, not execution. Where your goal needs built work, we will scope it separately under Services — retainer clients receive credits toward that work.

5. No-Guarantee Disclaimer

Sessions offer professional judgment, frameworks and lived experience — not guarantees of outcome. Decisions and their results remain yours. Nothing in a session constitutes legal, financial, medical, or investment advice.

6. Confidentiality

Session content is confidential. Written notes are yours alone. We will not reference you, your organization or your material in courses, books, posts, or marketing without your written permission. We may keep internal notes solely to serve you across sessions.

7. Your Content

You keep ownership of everything you bring to a session — documents, ideas, brands, music. We claim no rights in it, and any frameworks or materials of ours that we share remain ours, licensed to you for your own use, not for resale or redistribution.

8. Contact

Questions or scheduling changes: studio@gabochie.com · Book at studio.gabochie.com/consultation.
