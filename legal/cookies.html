Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2026

A cookie is a small text file a website asks your browser to store so the site can remember you between visits. This page lists every cookie set on studio.gabochie.com — ours and our providers’ — and how to control them. Our full data practices are in the Privacy Policy.

Cookies we set ourselves

We set no advertising, analytics, or cross-site tracking cookies. Only these strictly-necessary cookies:

NamePurposeExpires
ga_sessionKeeps you logged in to your dashboard and member workspace30 days
ga_upgradeTemporary flag confirming a membership tier upgrade while your dashboard refreshes60 seconds
ga_cookie_notice_dismissedRemembers that you dismissed the cookie notice (local storage, stays on your device)Until cleared
ga_utmRemembers which campaign referred you, so enrollments are attributed correctly (local storage)Until cleared

Cookies set by our providers

These companies set their own cookies under their own policies when you use their services on our site. We do not control them:

  • Cloudflare (hosting & security) — security cookies such as __cf_bm that distinguish real visitors from bots. Expire within 30 minutes to a day.
  • Flutterwave, Paystack & Moolre — checkout pages and embedded payment frames may set session and fraud-prevention cookies while you pay. See their respective privacy policies.
  • NOWPayments — crypto invoice pages may set session cookies while you complete a crypto payment.
  • Brevo — links in emails we send may carry tracking parameters so we know aggregate open and click rates.
  • YouTube & TikTok embeds — video players on course and article pages may set preferences and analytics cookies from Google/Meta. Videos only load their players when the page containing them is opened.

What we deliberately do not use

  • No advertising cookies or cross-site trackers.
  • No Meta Pixel, Google Analytics cookies, or TikTok pixels on our own pages.
  • Our on-site analytics are privacy-friendly and cookie-free.

Managing cookies

Because our own cookies are strictly necessary for login, blocking all cookies will sign you out — the site otherwise keeps working for reading articles and browsing courses. To control cookies: use your browser’s settings to block third-party cookies, clear site data for studio.gabochie.com, or browse in private/incognito mode. Provider checkout and video pages may require their cookies to function.

Changes

When we add or remove a cookie, we update this page and the date above.
