Partnership Terms

Last updated: October 2026 · Template — the signed agreement for each partnership always governs

These terms summarize how partnerships with Studio Gabochie work across our three levels — Community, Impact and Strategic. Every partnership is confirmed by a simple written agreement; where that agreement and this page differ, the agreement governs.

1. Partner Levels

Community Partner — free to join. Includes a unique referral link, a community discount code, shareable starter materials, and newsletter features at our discretion. No financial commitment either way.

Impact Partner — the partner sponsors one or more named scholarship cohorts (typically 25–100 seats). Cohort size, sponsor benefits and cost are sized to the partner's budget and recorded in the agreement.

Strategic Partner — annual, co-designed engagements: curriculum co-design, co-branded content, named program sponsorship and custom reporting. Scoped and priced per program.

2. Referral Rewards

  • Community (and higher) partners receive a unique referral link or code. When someone enrolls in a paid course or buys a resource through it, the partner earns a commission — a percentage of collected revenue, agreed in writing before launch.
  • Commissions are calculated on money actually received by us (net of payment-processor fees, refunds and chargebacks), and are paid monthly in arrears once the earned balance reaches GH¢200 or the partner requests payment below that threshold.
  • Self-referrals — the partner's own purchases through their own link — do not earn commissions.
  • Referral codes must not be advertised as discounts on coupon sites or used in a way that misrepresents our prices.

3. Sponsorship Cohorts

  • Sponsorship funds scholarship seats for learners; sponsors do not purchase course licenses for resale.
  • Sponsors may name the cohort (e.g. "The [Sponsor] Cohort") and receive impact reports with learner counts, enrollment, completion and progress statistics — always aggregated; individual learners are never identified to sponsors without the learner's consent.
  • If a sponsored cohort cannot be delivered (e.g. a program is discontinued), unspent sponsorship funds are refunded pro-rata or converted to future seats at the sponsor's choice.
  • Scholarship seats are generally valid for 12 months from issue unless the agreement states otherwise.

4. Co-Branded Content & Intellectual Property

  • We license partners a non-exclusive right to distribute the specific co-branded materials listed in the agreement, within the agreed community. Partners do not acquire ownership of the underlying courses, books, songs, artwork or code.
  • We retain full ownership of Studio Gabochie content, including the Guitar School, Love Of The Lord materials and all companion books. Partners' logos and brand assets remain theirs; each party uses the other's marks only as permitted in the agreement.
  • Original music in our programs is composed for Studio Gabochie. Partners may not add copyrighted third-party lyrics or melodies to program materials; public-domain and original works only.

5. Data & Privacy

Learner accounts belong to the learners. Sponsors receive aggregate impact reporting only, per Section 3. Our handling of personal data is described in the Privacy Policy; where a partner processes learner data, they do so as an independent controller under their own policies.

6. Conduct & Alignment

We partner with organizations that share our commitment to developing Christ-centered leaders who think clearly, create excellently, and serve faithfully. We may decline or end partnerships that conflict with this mission, and may remove a partner's codes and materials if the partnership is used in a way that harms learners or our reputation.

7. Term & Termination

Community partnerships run until either party ends them (written notice, effective immediately). Impact and Strategic partnerships run for the term in their agreement. Either party may end any partnership for material breach with 14 days' notice to fix it first. On termination, existing learners keep the access they were granted, accrued commissions are paid out, and co-branded materials are withdrawn per the agreement.

8. Contact

Questions about these terms: studio@gabochie.com · Start a partnership at studio.gabochie.com/partners.
