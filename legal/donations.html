Donation Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Your generosity makes the work of GideonAbochie Studio possible. This policy outlines how donations are handled.

Use of Funds

100% of donations are used to support the creation and distribution of books, videos, and teachings on creativity, love, and wisdom. This includes production costs, platform reach, and making resources freely available. We operate with minimal overhead and maximum transparency.

Refund Policy

Donations are non-refundable unless made in error (e.g., duplicate charge, incorrect amount). If you believe a donation was processed incorrectly, please contact us within 14 days and we will review the situation in good faith.

Receipts

All donations processed through Flutterwave, Ko-fi, and Buy Me a Coffee generate receipts from those platforms. Please retain these for your records. If you need a confirmation letter, contact us directly.

Recurring Donations

Monthly donations can be set up via Flutterwave. You may cancel at any time through the payment platform. We do not store recurring payment details.

Registration Status

GideonAbochie Studio is in the process of formal registration. Donations are currently received as personal contributions toward ministry work. Tax receipts may not be available until formal registration is complete. Consult your tax advisor for deductibility in your jurisdiction.

Contact

For donation-related inquiries: admin@gideonabochie.com
