Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Studio Gabochie (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over it — whether you are reading an article, taking a course, donating, or just visiting.

Who we are

Studio Gabochie is an education and media ministry based in Accra, Ghana, operating at studio.gabochie.com. For data-protection purposes we are the data controller for information collected through this website. Contact: studio@gabochie.com.

Information we collect

Account & learning. When you register, enroll in a course, join the guitar program, or upgrade a membership tier, we collect your name, email address, and phone number where you provide it, plus your course progress, quiz attempts, certificates earned, and membership tier and status.

Newsletter & WhatsApp updates. When you subscribe, we collect your name, email address, and — only if you give it — your phone number. WhatsApp messages are sent only where you have explicitly ticked the WhatsApp opt-in. Every marketing email contains an unsubscribe link; every WhatsApp message tells you how to stop them.

Donations & purchases. When you donate or pay for courses, books, tiers, merch, or guitar access, our payment providers (ExpressPay and Moolre, and NOWPayments for crypto) collect your name, email, phone number, and payment details directly on their own checkout pages. We never see or store your card numbers, bank details, or crypto private keys. We receive only your name, contact details, amount, currency, and transaction references so we can receipt your payment and unlock what you bought. Manual mobile-money and on-chain crypto payments you send to our public addresses are confirmed against the transaction reference you provide.

Contact & applications. Messages sent through our contact, partnership, speaking, tutoring, careers, and classifieds forms — including names, emails, phone numbers, CVs, and cover letters — are stored so we can respond.

Technical data. Like almost every website, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes IP addresses and basic request data to deliver pages securely and prevent abuse. Our analytics are privacy-friendly and cookie-free. If you enable them, embedded YouTube or TikTok players may set their own cookies — see our Cookie Policy.

Why we process your data (legal basis)

Consent — newsletter emails and WhatsApp messages, which you opt into and can withdraw at any time. Contract — creating your account, delivering courses and certificates, activating memberships, and fulfilling orders and donations. Legitimate interests — keeping the site secure, preventing fraud, and understanding in aggregate which content helps learners most. Legal obligation — keeping financial records where the law requires it.

Who we share data with

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information — ever. We share the minimum necessary with processors who help us operate, each bound by their own terms: Cloudflare (hosting, security, and database), Brevo (transactional and newsletter email), ExpressPay and Moolre (card, mobile-money, and bank payments), NOWPayments (crypto invoicing), and Meta/WhatsApp (only for messages you opted into). We disclose information only where required by Ghanaian law or to protect against fraud and abuse.

How long we keep data

Account and learning records are kept while your account is active and deleted on verified request. Marketing subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe, then suppressed from future sends. Donation and purchase invoices are retained for at least six years for financial record-keeping. Server and analytics logs are aggregated or expire automatically.

Your rights — Ghana & beyond

Under Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), you have the right to be informed about data we hold on you, to access it, to have inaccuracies corrected, to object to certain processing, and to request erasure where the law allows. Visitors from the EU/UK have equivalent rights under the GDPR/UK GDPR, including data portability and restriction of processing. To exercise any right, email studio@gabochie.com with the subject “Data request” — we respond within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with Ghana’s Data Protection Commission.

Cookies

We set no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies ourselves. A small number of strictly-necessary cookies keep you logged in and remember preferences; payment checkouts and embedded players may set their own. Full details: Cookie Policy.

Children

Our courses and content are intended for learners aged 13 and above. Younger learners should participate with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; contact us and we will delete any such data promptly.

Data security

All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Databases live behind authenticated Cloudflare infrastructure with rate-limited APIs, and payment credentials never touch our servers. No method of electronic transmission is 100% secure, but we treat your data the way we would want ours treated.

Changes to this policy

We update this page when our practices change, and revise the “last updated” date above. Material changes to marketing use will always ask for fresh consent rather than assuming it.

Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, data requests, or to withdraw consent: studio@gabochie.com.
