Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

GideonAbochie Studio ("we", "us", "our") operates news.gabochie.com (the "Newsletter") and studio.gabochie.com (the "Website"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information in compliance with the Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information You Provide:

  • Email address — collected when you subscribe to the newsletter or submit a classified ad.
  • Phone number — optionally collected on the subscribe form; required for classified listings.
  • Name — collected when you submit a classified ad.
  • Listing content — any information you voluntarily include in a classified ad (description, price, location, website, etc.).

Information Collected Automatically:

  • Usage data (pages visited, time spent, referring URL) via Cloudflare analytics.
  • IP address (anonymised where possible) for rate limiting and abuse prevention.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver the weekly newsletter to your email.
  • To review, publish, and manage classified listings.
  • To communicate with you about your listing status.
  • To improve our content and user experience.
  • To detect and prevent spam, fraud, or abuse.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data based on your consent (by subscribing or submitting a listing) and our legitimate interest in operating the newsletter and classifieds platform.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Cloudflare — for CDN, DDoS protection, and analytics.
  • Meta (WhatsApp) — if you opt into WhatsApp notifications, your phone number is processed via Meta's Cloud API.
  • Legal authorities — if required by law or to protect our rights.

Classified listings are published publicly. Contact information you include (email, phone) will be visible to all visitors.

5. Data Retention

We retain your subscription data until you unsubscribe. Classified listing data is retained for the duration of the listing plus 12 months for record-keeping. You may request deletion at any time.

6. Your Rights

Under the Ghana Data Protection Act, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data.
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent — unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time (link in every email).

To exercise any of these rights, email studio@gabochie.com.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit (TLS), rate limiting on API endpoints, and hashed storage of access credentials. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

8. Third-Party Links

Our newsletter and classifieds may contain links to external sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for users aged 15 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 15. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance.
