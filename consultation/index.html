Frequently Asked Questions
What does a session cost?
Quoted when we confirm your slot — by level, not by the minute. An Anchor Session costs less than the wrong decision it prevents; sprint and retainer pricing compounds the discount. You always have the number in writing before paying.
How is this different from the courses?
Courses teach the framework at your own pace; a consultation applies it to your situation, live. Many clients do both — take the course for depth, book a session for the stuck part.
How is this different from your Services?
Consultation is advice — thinking, mapping, deciding. Services are done-for-you work — builds, designs, curricula. Sprint and retainer clients get credits toward services when the thinking turns into work.
What if I need to reschedule?
Reschedule freely up to 24 hours before — the invoice carries to the new slot. Inside 24 hours or no-shows, the session is forfeited (see the Booking & Session Terms).
Do we meet in person or online?
Online by default — a video link comes with your confirmation. In-person sessions in Accra can be arranged at booking.
Is what we discuss confidential?
Yes. Session content stays between us; written notes go only to you. Nothing you share appears in courses, books or marketing without your written permission.
