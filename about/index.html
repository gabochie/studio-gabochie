Gideon Abochie

Founder, Studio Gabochie — Accra, Ghana

Gideon Abochie founded Studio Gabochie to equip a generation of African builders with three things school rarely teaches together: creative skill, character shaped by love, and wisdom rooted in Scripture.

He writes the Studio Weekly newsletter and books including The Bible as Kingdom OS, teaches systems thinking and creativity, leads the Love Of The Lord ministry, and builds practical courses — many starting free — for learners across Ghana and beyond.
