The Vision

Creativity, Love & Wisdom — Rooted in Scripture

GideonAbochie Studio was born from a deep conviction: the Church needs a fresh encounter with the God of creativity, the God of love, and the God of wisdom. These are not abstract concepts — they are the very nature of God revealed in Scripture.

"In the beginning, God created" (Gen 1:1). "God is love" (1 John 4:8). "The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom" (Prov 9:10). Every book, every video, every teaching points back to these three pillars — because when we create like Him, love like Him, and walk in His wisdom, we reflect His image to the world.

Through TikTok, books, and teaching, we're reaching a generation that is hungry for truth but drowning in noise. The message is simple: you were created to create, you were made to love, and you are called to walk in wisdom.

The Three Pillars

Creativity · Love · Wisdom

Three truths that shape everything we teach, create, and share. Rooted in Scripture, expressed through creativity, and lived out in community.

Creativity

We are made in the image of the Creator. Creativity is our birthright — whether through design, writing, or other creative work. We teach creative excellence as an act of worship and a tool for transformation.

Love

Love is not a feeling — it's a covenant commitment. We teach the radical, sacrificial love of God and call a generation to love God with all their heart and love their neighbor as themselves.

Wisdom

The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom. In an age of information without understanding, we teach biblical wisdom for every area of life — relationships, work, creativity, and purpose.

Scripture-Based

Every teaching, every resource, every creative work flows from the Word of God. The Bible is not a relic — it is the operating system for the Kingdom, alive and active today.
