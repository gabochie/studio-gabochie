Peace to you. Here are this week's three questions from the community — answered honestly, from the Word. Take the one that speaks to your week and sit with it.
Purpose
Is it wrong to be ambitious and want to succeed?
Ambition is not sin — selfish ambition is. Work heartily as unto the Lord. Let your success serve people and give God glory, not become your god.
Colossians 3:23 · Proverbs 4:7

Faith
Why does God feel silent when I need Him most?
Silence is not absence. God often quiets us to deepen us. Stay in the Word, keep praying short honest prayers, and trust what He already said even when you cannot feel Him.
Isaiah 41:10 · Psalm 46:10
Watch answer 2 →
Work & Money
I prayed for money for school and got nothing. Where is God in my lack?
Sometimes God provides the door before the money. Ask Him for wisdom and one next step this week — a skill, a person, a small job. Provision often arrives through a step, not a lightning bolt.
Philippians 4:19 · Matthew 6:33
