## word
Refresh today: Think on These Things. Open your Bible to Philippians 4, verse 8. Finally, brethren, whatever things are true, whatever things are noble, whatever things are just, whatever things are pure, whatever things are lovely, whatever things are of good report, if there is any virtue and if there is anything praiseworthy — meditate on these things. God gave you a thought menu. Most people eat garbage and wonder why the soul is sick.

Walk through the eight filters slowly, like testing water. Is it TRUE — or rumor, exaggeration, fear-dressed-as-fact? Is it NOBLE — worthy of a King's child, or cheap gossip? JUST — fair to all, or slanted for my side? PURE — clean, or does it stain on contact? LOVELY — beautiful, bridge-building, or ugly and dividing? GOOD REPORT — would you repeat it proudly, or only in whispers? VIRTUE — excellent, lifting, or mediocre rot? PRAISEWORTHY — does it turn hearts Godward? Run every show, song, chat, and headline through these eight gates. Most fail at gate one — let them starve outside.

But note: meditate ON these things. Not merely avoid evil — FILL with good. An empty swept mind invites seven worse tenants. Nature abhors a vacuum and so does the soul. Replace, don't just remove: swap the fear scroll for one psalm, the gossip call for one encouragement, the envy stare for one thanksgiving. Fill the house with music and there is no room for noise.

For thoughts are prayers before they are prayers — what you rehearse, you become. Anxious rehearsals produce anxious lives; grateful rehearsals produce grateful ones. And the promise follows in verse 9: do these things, and the God of PEACE will be with you. Right thinking births right feeling. Peace is downstream of meditation.

Your step: audit one input this week — unfollow, mute, or limit one fear/gossip source — and memorize Philippians 4:8 word-perfect. Each morning, pick one of the eight and hunt it deliberately all day. Tell one person your filter.

Father, I present my mind to You. Guard its gates with Your eight filters, fill it with what is true and lovely, and let Your peace stand watch over my thoughts. In Jesus' name. Amen.
