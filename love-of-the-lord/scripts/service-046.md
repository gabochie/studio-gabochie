## word
Refresh today: Hope That Does Not Disappoint — shameless, patient, rejoicing hope. Open your Bible to Romans 5, verse 5. Weeks ago we anchored hope and traced its chain. Today: how hope BEHAVES — unashamed before mockers, patient through delays, rejoicing ahead of arrivals.

SHAMELESS hope. Hope does not disappoint — literally, does not put to shame. Mockers sneer: where is your God now? Still single? Still renting? Still waiting? Shameless hope answers with head high: my God is coming, and His lateness is not His absence. Refuse to apologize for believing. Noah built shamelessly for decades; the ark mocked until rain. Your hope will look foolish right up until it looks prophetic. Hold the posture; heaven keeps the schedule.

PATIENT hope — hope as a helmet. Paul elsewhere calls hope the helmet of salvation: head protection for long battles. Helmets are worn BEFORE wounds, daily, as discipline. Patient hope shows up every morning dressed for a wait that may last years: still praying, still sowing, still pure, still giving. Impatience strips the helmet mid-battle and wonders at the headache. Keep it on. The patient inherit promises; the hasty inherit regrets.

REJOICING hope — rejoicing IN hope, as Romans 12 commands. Rejoice NOW over THEN: celebrate the coming harvest at sowing time, sing over the answer before it lands, thank God for the testimony in advance. This is not denial; it is time travel by faith — future joy imported into present pressure. Make a list titled "Coming" and rejoice over each item aloud this week. Joy is hope's native language; speak it fluently.

Your step: answer one mocker (including inner ones) with one shameless declaration; put your helmet on with one patient practice renewed; and rejoice aloud over three coming things daily. Tell one person your hope-list.

Father, my hope will not shame me — it is patient as a helmet and joyful as a foretaste. Dress me daily, keep me rejoicing, and bring every arrival in Your time. In Jesus' name. Amen.
