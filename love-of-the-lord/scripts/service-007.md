## word
Refresh today: Grace for New Beginnings — again, and deeper. Open your Bible to Lamentations 3:22-23. Because of the Lord's great love we are not consumed. Seven weeks into this year of grace, we return to where we started — not because we failed, but because grace has more rooms.

First: His love is STEADFAST — the Hebrew word is covenant love, the love that signed its name. Feelings come and go; covenant stays. God is not gracious to you the way weather is nice to you — sometimes. He is gracious the way a foundation holds a house — always, underneath everything. When your performance wobbles, His covenant does not. Stand on what He signed, not on how you feel.

Second: we are NOT CONSUMED. Look at that phrase. With all you have done and left undone, you should have been consumed long ago — and you were not. That survival is not luck and not your strength. It is steadfast love absorbing what should have destroyed you. Count it: the sickness that turned, the accident that missed, the mistake that did not end you. Each one is a receipt of grace. Keep them. Review them when doubt knocks.

Third: grace must move from VISITED to INHABITED. Many taste grace on Sundays and live by guilt on Mondays. That is like owning a house and sleeping outside. Move in. When guilt speaks, answer with the covenant: I am loved, I am kept, I begin again. Grace is not a holiday destination; it is your home address.

Your step: write down three "not consumed" moments from your own life — three times you should have fallen and did not. Read them aloud as testimonies this week, and tell one person the third one. Grace shared multiplies.

Father, Your steadfast love is my foundation and Your faithfulness my song. I move into grace as my home, not my holiday. Keep me, and make me a keeper of others. In Jesus' name. Amen.
