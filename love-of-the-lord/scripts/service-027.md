## word
Refresh today, opening our third quarter: Love Your Neighbour. Open your Bible to Mark 12, verse 31. You shall love your neighbor as yourself. Welcome to Love in Action — where the grace we received and the Kingdom we sought now walk out the door dressed as love.

A lawyer once asked Jesus: who IS my neighbor? Jesus answered with a Samaritan — the wrong tribe, on the wrong road — who stopped, bandaged, oiled, lifted, paid, and promised to return. Notice what made him a neighbor: not proximity (priest and Levite were nearer), not tribe (he was the outsider), but MERCY IN MOTION. Your neighbor is not who lives closest; it is who needs you most when you have the chance to help. Proximity plus need equals neighbor.

And love here is a VERB with receipts: oil and wine (first aid), his own animal (transport), two denarii (money), time (stayed), and a return promise (follow-up). Love that costs nothing changes nothing. Check your love's receipts this week: what did it spend — time, money, comfort, reputation? Feeling warmly toward someone is not loving them. Love pays, stays, and returns.

So name ONE neighbor today — not humanity, a name. The lonely elder on your street, the struggling single mother, the newcomer at work, the watchman you never greet. Then do one Samaritan act before Friday: visit, give, help, pray WITH them (not just for them). Small acts, done consistently, are how neighborhoods become Kingdoms.

Father, open my eyes to my neighbor — the need near me I keep passing. Give me oil, wine, time, and a returning heart. Make my love a verb with receipts this week. In Jesus' name. Amen.
