## word
Refresh today: Love Your Neighbour — beginning with yourself, ending with your enemies. Open your Bible to Mark 12, verse 31. You shall love your neighbor AS YOURSELF. Weeks ago we learned mercy in motion. Today: the two neglected ends of the command — the SELF you starve and the ENEMY you exclude.

As YOURSELF. You cannot pour from an empty calabash. Many burn out loving others while hating themselves — skipping rest, skipping meals, skipping the doctor, calling it sacrifice. That is not holiness; it is slow suicide that orphans your calling. Love yourself RIGHTLY: rest without guilt (even God rested), eat and sleep as stewardship, forgive yourself as fully as God forgave you, speak to yourself the way you speak to struggling friends. A well-loved self loves others well; a starved self resents them. Receive love before you retail it.

Then let neighbour-love become WITNESS. They will know by love — so love visibly, specifically, repeatedly. The neighbour who watches your consistency across months is being evangelized without a single tract. Keep showing up: greet, help, remember names, celebrate milestones, mourn losses. Evangelism is 90% love with a 10% explanation at the end. Earn the hearing, then give the reason.

And finally: ENEMIES. Jesus commands love of enemies — bless those who curse, pray for persecutors. The rival at work, the abusive ex, the tribal bigot, the online mocker. Loving enemies does not mean trusting them (wisdom keeps gates); it means willing their good and praying their blessing. Start with one prayer for one enemy daily this week. Watch your heart unclench and, in time, watch God work in theirs.

Your step: do one healthy self-love act (rest, checkup, Sabbath hour), one neighbour act, and one enemy prayer — all three this week. Write them, tell one person, do them before Friday.

Father, teach me to love myself rightly, my neighbour visibly, and my enemies prayerfully. Fill me first, then flow through me to all. In Jesus' name. Amen.
