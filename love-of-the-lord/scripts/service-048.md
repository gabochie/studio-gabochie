## word
Full sermon today: Faith in the Waiting — strengthened hearts that outlast delays. Open your Bible to Psalm 27, verse 14. Wait on the Lord; be of good courage, and He shall strengthen your heart; wait, I say, on the Lord! Weeks ago we learned active, courageous, repeated waiting. Today, first Sunday of September, we go all the way in: HOW He strengthens, WHO waited well, and WHEN waiting ends in glory.

How He STRENGTHENS: courage here is heart-strength, and God supplies it three ways. Through His WORD — promises eaten daily become backbone; find your waiting verse and chew it till courage comes. Through His SPIRIT — who strengthens with might in the inner man; ask daily for fresh filling, not yesterday's leftovers. Through His PEOPLE — courage is contagious and discouragement too; walk with waiters who worship, not whiners who wilt. Plug into all three streams this week and watch fainting flee.

Who waited WELL: David — anointed king as a boy, appointed years later, with caves, betrayals, and refused shortcuts between. Twice he held Saul's life in his hands (the robe's hem, the spear and jug) and twice refused to seize what God had promised to GIVE. Learn it: never steal in panic what God promised in patience. Shortcuts cost thrones; waiting inherits them. Your cave season is not wasted — it is crown preparation. Refuse the hem-cutting options this week.

When waiting ENDS: it ends in STRENGTH now and GLORY then. Strength now — He SHALL strengthen your heart (promise, not maybe). Glory then — they that wait upon the Lord shall renew strength, mount up, run untired, walk unfainting. Eagles, not chickens, are the end of waiters. And note the REFRAIN closing the verse: wait, I SAY, on the Lord — David repeats himself because waiting fails once and must resume. Relapse into fretting? Return to waiting. Again. Still. Until glory.

And if your wait has a NAME today — healing, husband, house, harvest, help — bring it to this altar in a moment. We will wait on Him together, and He will strengthen every heart present. No one waits alone here.

Your step this month: name your wait, attach your waiting verse to it, join one waiter-who-worships, and refuse one shortcut. Tell one person all three.

Father, I wait on You — actively, courageously, repeatedly. Strengthen my heart by Word, Spirit, and saints. Make my cave a crown-preparation, and end my waiting in glory. In Jesus' mighty name. Amen.
