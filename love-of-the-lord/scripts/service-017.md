## word
Refresh today: Kingdom Ambition. Open your Bible to Mark 10, verses 43 to 45. Whoever desires to become great among you shall be your servant. And whoever of you desires to be first shall be slave of all. For even the Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give His life a ransom for many. Ambition is not the problem. Its DIRECTION is.

James and John wanted thrones — one at His right, one at His left. Jesus did not rebuke the WANT; He redirected it. Want much! Want influence, want platforms, want nations! But want them FOR many, not FROM many. Sanctified ambition asks: who will be blessed if I rise? Selfish ambition asks: who must fall? Check your dreams by their beneficiaries.

For in the Kingdom, greatness is measured in TOWELS, not titles. Whoever desires to become great shall be your SERVANT. The world says: get people to serve you and you have arrived. Jesus says: serve people and you have arrived. The executive who washes feet outranks the chairman who demands it. Leadership in God's Kingdom is not chop-chop — eat first, biggest portion — it is serve-first: the leader eats last and ensures all are fed.

And the pattern is Christ Himself: He did not come to BE served, but TO serve — and to GIVE His life. The highest throne in the universe belongs to the lowest Servant. So stop waiting for a position to start serving. Serve where you stand: that junior colleague, that aging parent, that church chair, that neighbor's errand. Thrones chase servants in the Kingdom; they flee the self-seeking.

Your step: perform one deliberate act of service this week for someone who cannot repay you — and tell no one but God and one witness of your growth. Let heaven record what earth ignores.

Father, baptize my ambition. Make me great in serving and first in enslaving myself to love, following Jesus who gave His life for me. In His name. Amen.
