## word
Full sermon today, first Sunday of October, closing our year: Hope That Does Not Disappoint — reviewed, ultimate, and commissioned. Open your Bible to Romans 5, verse 5. Now hope does not disappoint, because the love of God has been poured out in our hearts by the Holy Spirit who was given to us. Fifty-two Sundays ago we began with grace. Today we end with hope — tested a full year, proven every week, and pointing beyond the year itself.

REVIEW with me, year in hand. Q1, Foundations of Grace: new beginnings from ruins, mercy removing and pitying, years restored, frustration turned to seeking, roots built and walked, mercies morning-renewed, covenant receipts kept. Q2, The Kingdom Mind: order before provision, treasure heavenward, minds washed, ambition towelled, colonies planted, thoughts filtered, righteousness pathed, masters chosen, sacrifice proved, ransom hiddenness, eager waiting, peace guarding. Q3, Love in Action: neighbours named, forgiveness seventied, service worshipped, houses choosing, generosity mechanized, answers gentled, selves rightly loved, debts released, altars daily, habits dutiful. Q4, Hope and Harvest: anchors cast, harvests lawfully expected, waits actively kept, thanks warred, fires tended, conclusions simplified, hopes shamelessed, sowings calendared, strengths renewed, blessings counted. Fifty-two weeks, one thread: great is His faithfulness — morning by morning new mercies we have seen.

But ULTIMATE hope looks BEYOND the year. Our citizenship finale is not a calendar but a Coming: eagerly waiting the Savior who will transform these lowly bodies like His glory. Every hope kept this year was rehearsal; the wedding itself awaits. Death, which ended many stories this year, ends none of ours — resurrection rewrites every grave into a doorway. Lift eyes past October: the best year is not behind (2026-27 was glorious) but AHEAD — the year with no tears, no temple needed, no night. Hope that far outshines hope that merely copes.

And now COMMISSIONED into Year Two. Hope received becomes hope CARRIED: to the hopeless ward, the grieving home, the addicted street, the anxious campus, the nations unreached. You are now veterans of hope — fifty-two weeks trained. Enlist afresh today: one person to disciple through next year, one place to carry fire, one promise to stand on publicly. The year ends; the assignment continues. Bringers bring; hopers herald.

If you have never given your life to Jesus — what better morning than the year's close? Every hope preached fifty-two weeks stands in one Person with open arms. Come home. And family: finish grateful, finish certain, finish sent. New year soon, same faithful God, fresh mercies already issued.

Father, fifty-two weeks prove it: hope in You does not disappoint. We close this year reviewing Your faithfulness, eyes fixed beyond it on resurrection morning, commissioned as carriers into Year Two. Bless every house, establish every work, and let the coming year outshine this one. Go with us, now and forever. In the mighty name of Jesus. Amen.
