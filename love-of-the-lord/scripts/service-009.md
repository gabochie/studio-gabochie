## word
Full sermon today: The God Who Restores — and He restores to OVERFLOW. Open your Bible to Joel chapter 2. Weeks ago we learned He restores the years. Today, first Sunday of December, we learn He restores beyond the loss — until shame itself is gone and the Spirit falls.

Read verse 24 with me: The threshing floors shall be full of wheat, and the vats shall overflow with new wine and oil. Do you see it? God does not merely refill what the locusts ate. He fills to OVERFLOWING. Restoration in God's economy is not break-even; it is abundance. The delicate balance you lost becomes a surplus. The years the locusts ate return with interest. Expect not just recovery — expect overflow. Pray bigger than replacement.

Then verse 26: You shall eat in plenty and be satisfied, and praise the name of the Lord your God, who has dealt wondrously with you; and My people shall never be put to shame. Never be put to shame! Shame is the locusts' cruelest crop — the whisper that your past disqualifies your future. God answers shame directly: restored people are shameless people, not because they hide the past but because grace rewrote it. Your testimony will embarrass the devil, not you. Stop letting people who knew your yesterday veto your tomorrow. Heaven has overruled.

And then the crown, verses 28 and 29: I will pour out My Spirit on all flesh. Restoration's goal was never just full barns — it is full HEARTS. God restores fortune to restore FELLOWSHIP. The ultimate proof you are restored is not what is in your hands but Who is in your heart: sons and daughters prophesying, old men dreaming, young men seeing visions. Ask today not only for things restored, but for the Spirit poured. Things satisfy for a season; the Spirit satisfies forever.

So where are you? If locusts are still eating — return today; rend your heart, and watch Him act. If shoots are appearing — water them with obedience and thanksgiving; do not abandon the field halfway. If vats are overflowing — testify loudly and give generously, for overflow is given to be shared. And all of us: ask for the outpouring. Lord, pour Your Spirit on me, my house, this ministry, this nation.

Your step this month, our giving month: write down one area where you will believe God for OVERFLOW, not mere recovery — and sow one seed toward it this week, whether money, time, or forgiveness. Overflow flows toward sowers.

Father of restoration and God of overflow, remove every shame, fill every vat, and pour Your Spirit on all flesh — beginning with me, today. Make my restored life a river for others. In the mighty name of Jesus. Amen.
