## word
Refresh today, opening our new quarter: Seek First the Kingdom. Open your Bible to Matthew 6:33. But seek first the kingdom of God and His righteousness, and all these things shall be added to you. Welcome to The Kingdom Mind — thirteen weeks on thinking, living, and thriving God's way.

What does SEEK FIRST mean? It means order, not abandonment. Jesus is not telling students to drop school or traders to close shop. He is telling us what comes FIRST: before profit, principle; before pressure, prayer; before the deal, the deed that is right. First means first — first hour of the day, first portion of income, first filter on every big decision: does this advance His rule or only my comfort?

What is the KINGDOM? Wherever God reigns. And note the promise attached to His reign: all THESE things shall be added — food, drink, clothing, the very things worriers chase. Provision follows priority. The pagans run after things; sons receive things running after them. Worry is practical atheism — acting as if Father does not know or care. He knows. He cares. Seek Him first and watch additions follow.

And His RIGHTEOUSNESS — right living as the path, not the price. You do not buy blessing with behavior; you walk in blessing by walking right. Integrity is the currency of the Kingdom: pay your debts, keep your word, refuse the shortcut. Righteousness positions you where additions can find you.

Your step: choose one FIRST this week — first hour given to God before phone, first portion of income set aside, or one decision filtered by Kingdom-first. Write it, tell one person, do it before Friday.

Father, I seek Your Kingdom first — Your rule over my time, money, and choices, and Your righteousness as my path. Add what I need as I put You first. In Jesus' name. Amen.
