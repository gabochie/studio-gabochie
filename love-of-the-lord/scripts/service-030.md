## word
Full sermon today: Marriage and Family — choose this day. Open your Bible to Joshua 24, verse 15. Choose for yourselves this day whom you will serve... But as for me and my house, we will serve the Lord. Old Joshua, eyebrows of experience, lays the choice bare: drift is also a decision — and he has made his. Today every house here makes its own.

Choose THIS DAY. Not someday when money settles, children grow, or tempers cool. This day. Drift chooses for you if you refuse to choose: unled children follow streets, unguarded marriages follow feelings, prayerless homes follow noise. Decision day must come, and it had better come today, out loud, on purpose. Gather your house this week — physically, around a table or a phone call — and say it together: as for US, we will serve the Lord.

As for ME AND MY HOUSE. Mark the order: me FIRST, then my house. You cannot lead where you will not go. Fathers, mothers, eldest, guardians — your private devotion sets your house's temperature. Children read lives louder than lectures. Be the first Bible your house reads: pray where they see, forgive where they hear, worship where they watch. Headship is not boss-ship; it is first-to-kneel servant leadership that makes following natural.

Build the FAMILY ALTAR. A house that prays together stays together — not as slogan but as structure. Ten minutes: one verse read aloud, each voice praying one sentence, one song if you sing. Daily is ideal; thrice weekly is a start; zero is a slow leak. Begin this week, however clumsily. God honors stammering family altars more than silent perfect intentions.

For MARRIAGE: leave, cleave, become one. Leave every other loyalty that rivals your spouse — parents, friends, work, phone. Cleave — glue yourselves deliberately through talk, touch, laughter, and repair after fights (never sleep warring). Husbands, love as Christ loved — nourishing, cherishing, dying to self daily. Wives, respect and receive — honor that crowns effort. Singles: prepare now what you will practice then; purity today is marital capital tomorrow.

For PARENTING: train UP the child in the way HE should go — study each child (they differ!), discipline consistently without anger, delight loudly and often. Children spelled backward need LOVE forward: listen, overlook small, value always, enjoy deliberately. Your words are framing their inner voice for life — frame grace.

And we dedicate today. In a moment we will pray over every marriage and every home represented — the strong to be strengthened, the strained to be healed, the single to be prepared, the broken to be rebuilt. No perfect families here; only choosing ones. Choose this day.

Father, as for me and my house, we will serve You. Build our family altar, heal our marriages, train our children, and make our homes embassies of heaven. We choose You — today, together, forever. In Jesus' name. Amen.
