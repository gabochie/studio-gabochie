## word
Full sermon today, first Sunday of April, closing our second quarter: Seek First the Kingdom — from SEEKING to ESTABLISHING. Open your Bible to Matthew 6:33, and hear Matthew 6:10 beside it: Your kingdom come. Your will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Thirteen weeks ago we began seeking. Today we end the quarter ESTABLISHING — no longer only asking the Kingdom to come to us, but carrying it to others.

For there are two movements in this verse most miss. SEEK first — the pursuit, the priority, the daily firstfruits we have practiced all quarter. But the Lord's Prayer reveals the goal: Your KINGDOM COME — not only into my heart, but into my house, street, office, and nation. Seekers become bringers. You came to church to GET the Kingdom; you leave to TAKE it somewhere. This is graduation: from consumer of grace to carrier of reign.

Now count with me, quarter in review. Week 14: order — Kingdom first in time, money, decisions. Week 15: treasure — hearts following heavenward funds. Week 16: the mold refused and the mind washed. Week 17: ambition sanctified into towels. Week 18: colony living as heaven's ambassadors. Week 19: eight filters for holy thinking. Week 20: righteousness as the landing strip. Week 21: one Master, contentment, open hands. Weeks 22 to 25: sacrifice and proof and stands; ransom and hiddenness; eager waiting and weeping hearts; peace that guards. Thirteen Sundays, one reign. If you missed weeks, the archive keeps them — the Kingdom has no expiry date.

And now we are SENT into Love in Action — for a Kingdom established must be a Kingdom EXPRESSED. Love your neighbor, forgive freers, serve as worship: next quarter we turn reign into relationship. So today: thank Him aloud for three Kingdom-additions of this quarter — provisions, protections, promotions of the soul. Release Q2 fully and step into Q3 commissioned. And invite one person into next quarter with you — bringers bring.

If you have never given your life to Jesus — what better morning than a quarter-close? The altar will be open in a moment. Come under the reign today. And if you drifted, hear this: the same Kingdom that sought you for thirteen weeks seeks you still. New quarter, same King, fresh orders.

Father, Your Kingdom come — in me first, then through me to my house, street, and nation. I close this quarter grateful and open the next commissioned. Establish Your reign in every room I enter. In the mighty name of Jesus. Amen.
