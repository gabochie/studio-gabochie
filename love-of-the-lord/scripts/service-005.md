## word
Refresh today: Rooted and Established. Open your Bible to Colossians chapter 2, verses 6 and 7. As you received Christ Jesus the Lord, so walk in Him, rooted and built up in Him and established in the faith, just as you were taught, abounding in thanksgiving. A tree is only as strong as what nobody sees. Today God checks our roots.

Point one: be ROOTED — depth before height. Nobody applauds roots, but everybody enjoys shade. Roots grow in the secret place: the Word you read when no one posts it, the prayer you pray when no one hears, the obedience that costs you something. If your Christian life is all leaves and no roots — all performance, no secret place — the first dry season will embarrass you. Go deep this week: ten minutes in the Word daily, no phone, no show.

Point two: be BUILT UP — growth is construction, not accident. Paul mixes metaphors on purpose: a tree AND a building. Line upon line, brick upon brick. Sunday service is a brick. Monday devotional is a brick. Module One of the school is a brick. Nobody drifts into maturity; you are built by what you repeatedly do. Ask yourself: what did I build this week — and what did I merely wish?

Point three: be ESTABLISHED — stability that sings. Established means storms find you still standing AND still singing, for Paul ends with abounding in thanksgiving. Gratitude is not the decoration of the rooted life; it is the nourishment. A complaining Christian withers from the inside. Start a thanksgiving list: three things daily. Watch your roots drink.

Here is your step. Choose one root habit and begin it this week: ten minutes of Word every morning, plus this Sunday service, plus three thanksgivings a day. Tell one person your habit so your roots have a witness. Small roots, faithfully watered, hold up great trees.

Father, make me rooted when nobody claps and established when storms come. Build me line upon line, and fill my mouth with thanksgiving. In Jesus' name. Amen.
