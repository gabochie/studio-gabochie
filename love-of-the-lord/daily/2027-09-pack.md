# 2027-09 — Heart Renovated — First Commandment

Yearly message: Focus on The Love of The Lord. Focus: Return to first love.

## Format 1 — Daily App / Audio (30 days)

### 2027-09-01 (Wed) — Physical Strength & Endurance [service-047 Sowing and Reaping]

**Scripture:** Matthew 22:37 — Love the Lord with all.

**Teaching:** Primary pursuit. Month focus — Heart Renovated — First Commandment: Return to first love. This week we carry "Sowing and Reaping" (Galatians 6:7). Physical Strength & Endurance flows from being loved first. Let Matthew 22:37 settle you: Love the Lord with all.

**Prayer:** Heavenly Father, we thank You for this brand-new day and the opportunity to gather as family in Your presence. Your mercies are new every morning. Forgive us our sins and trespasses, and we forgive all who trespassed against us. We stand cleansed by the blood of Jesus. Strengthen this temple. Give me vitality and endurance to run my race to the crown.

**Declaration:** My body is the temple of the Holy Spirit; divine health is my portion.

**Decree:** My body is the temple of the Holy Spirit; divine health is my portion. Love the Lord with all. In Jesus’ name, Amen.

**Action:** Walk 10 minutes praying the declaration; honour rest and water today.
