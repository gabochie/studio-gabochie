# 2027-02 — Hesed — Covenant Love

Yearly message: Focus on The Love of The Lord. Focus: Steadfast love that keeps promises.

## Format 1 — Daily App / Audio (28 days)

### 2027-02-01 (Mon) — Spiritual Strength & Growth [service-017 Kingdom Ambition]

**Scripture:** Psalm 136:1-3 — His love endures forever.

**Teaching:** Covenant love outlasts moods. Month focus — Hesed — Covenant Love: Steadfast love that keeps promises. This week we carry "Kingdom Ambition" (Mark 10:43-45). Spiritual Strength & Growth flows from being loved first. Let Psalm 136:1-3 settle you: His love endures forever.

**Prayer:** Heavenly Father, we thank You for this brand-new day and the opportunity to gather as family in Your presence. Your mercies are new every morning. Forgive us our sins and trespasses, and we forgive all who trespassed against us. We stand cleansed by the blood of Jesus. Fill me with hunger for Your Word. Let my faith rise, my prayer catch fire, and my roots go deep in Your love.

**Declaration:** I am rooted and established in the eternal love of God.

**Decree:** I am rooted and established in the eternal love of God. His love endures forever. In Jesus’ name, Amen.

**Action:** Read your day scripture aloud 3x and write one obedience step.
