# 2027-04 — The Cross — Ultimate Demonstration

Yearly message: Focus on The Love of The Lord. Focus: Gave Himself for us.

## Format 1 — Daily App / Audio (30 days)

### 2027-04-01 (Thu) — Deeper Love for All Humanity [service-025 Think on These Things]

**Scripture:** John 3:16 — For God so loved the world.

**Teaching:** Giving is proof. Month focus — The Cross — Ultimate Demonstration: Gave Himself for us. This week we carry "Think on These Things" (Philippians 4:8). Deeper Love for All Humanity flows from being loved first. Let John 3:16 settle you: For God so loved the world.

**Prayer:** Heavenly Father, we thank You for this brand-new day and the opportunity to gather as family in Your presence. Your mercies are new every morning. Forgive us our sins and trespasses, and we forgive all who trespassed against us. We stand cleansed by the blood of Jesus. Shed Your love abroad in my heart. Let me see people through the eyes of Jesus.

**Declaration:** I am a conduit of God’s love to humanity today.

**Decree:** I am a conduit of God’s love to humanity today. For God so loved the world. In Jesus’ name, Amen.

**Action:** Do one deliberate kindness for someone unlovable or overlooked.
