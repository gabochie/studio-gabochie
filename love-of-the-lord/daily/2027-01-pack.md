# 2027-01 — The Eternal Source (Pre-creation love)

Yearly message: Focus on The Love of The Lord. Focus: Loved before you started; love is who God is.

## Format 1 — Daily App / Audio (31 days)

### 2027-01-01 (Fri) — Wisdom for the Abundant Life [service-012 His Mercies Are New]

**Scripture:** 1 John 4:8, 16 — God is love. Whoever lives in love lives in God, and God in them.

**Teaching:** Love is not what God does; it is who He is. Month focus — The Eternal Source (Pre-creation love): Loved before you started; love is who God is. This week we carry "His Mercies Are New" (Lamentations 3:23). Wisdom for the Abundant Life flows from being loved first. Let 1 John 4:8, 16 settle you: God is love. Whoever lives in love lives in God, and God in them.

**Prayer:** Heavenly Father, we thank You for this brand-new day and the opportunity to gather as family in Your presence. Your mercies are new every morning. Forgive us our sins and trespasses, and we forgive all who trespassed against us. We stand cleansed by the blood of Jesus. Give me wisdom to steward resources well. Break scarcity; teach me generosity.

**Declaration:** I walk in Kingdom abundance to be a blessing.

**Decree:** I walk in Kingdom abundance to be a blessing. God is love. Whoever lives in love lives in God, and God in them. In Jesus’ name, Amen.

**Action:** Review one money choice; give or save as worship today.
