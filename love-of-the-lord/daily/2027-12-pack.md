## Format 1 — Daily App / Audio (31 days)

### 2027-12-01 (Wed) — Physical Strength & Endurance [service-045 The End of the Matter]

**Scripture:** Romans 5:5 — Hope does not disappoint.

**Teaching:** Poured-out love, poured-in hope. Month focus — Triumphant Love — Restoration & Eternity: Hope, harvest, thanksgiving. This week we carry "The End of the Matter" (Ecclesiastes 12:13). Physical Strength & Endurance flows from being loved first. Let Romans 5:5 settle you: Hope does not disappoint.

Give me vitality and endurance to run my race to the crown.

**Decree:** My body is the temple of the Holy Spirit; divine health is my portion. Hope does not disappoint. In Jesus’ name, Amen.
