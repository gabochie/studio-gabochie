### 2027-05-01 (Sat) — Spiritual Warfare (Praying the Scriptures) [service-029 Serving as Worship]

**Scripture:** 1 John 3:1 — That we should be called children of God.

**Teaching:** Lavished belonging. Month focus — The Fatherhouse — Adoption & Identity: Called children of God. This week we carry "Serving as Worship" (Romans 12:1). Spiritual Warfare (Praying the Scriptures) flows from being loved first. Let 1 John 3:1 settle you: That we should be called children of God.

**Prayer:** Heavenly Father, we thank You for this brand-new day and the opportunity to gather as family in Your presence. Your mercies are new every morning. Forgive us our sins and trespasses, and we forgive all who trespassed against us. We stand cleansed by the blood of Jesus. I put on the whole armour of God. No weapon formed against me shall prosper (Isaiah 54:17). I trample all the power of the enemy (Luke 10:19) and pull down every stronghold (2 Corinthians 10:4-5). Angels guard me in all my ways (Psalm 91:11).

**Declaration:** I enforce the victory of the cross over my life and family today.

**Decree:** I enforce the victory of the cross over my life and family today. That we should be called children of God. In Jesus’ name, Amen.

**Action:** Speak Ephesians 6:10-11 aloud; cancel one fear by name.
