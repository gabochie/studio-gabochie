Loved before you started

1 John 4:8, 16 · Grace for New Beginnings (service-013)
"God is love. Whoever lives in love lives in God, and God in them."

Teaching (2 min read): Love is not what God does; it is who He is. Before creation, before your performance, He was love. This week we carry "Grace for New Beginnings" (Lamentations 3:22-23): eternal love is the source that makes seeking the Kingdom possible. Wisdom for the Abundant Life flows from being loved first — not striving to be loved.

Prayer: Heavenly Father, we thank You for this brand-new day and the opportunity to gather as family in Your presence. Your mercies are new every morning. Forgive us our sins and trespasses, and we forgive all who trespassed against us. We stand cleansed by the blood of Jesus. Give me wisdom to steward resources well. Break scarcity; teach me generosity.

"I walk in Kingdom abundance to be a blessing."

Decree (speak aloud): I walk in Kingdom abundance to be a blessing. God is love. Whoever lives in love lives in God, and God in them. In Jesus' name, Amen.

Action step: Review one money choice; give or save as worship today.
