# 2027-06 — Love vs Law — Understanding Grace

Yearly message: Focus on The Love of The Lord. Focus: Obedience proves love; ritual cannot replace it.

## Format 1 — Daily App / Audio (30 days)

### 2027-06-01 (Tue) — Mental Maturity & Clarity [service-034 Forgiveness That Frees]

**Scripture:** John 14:15 — If you love me, keep my commands.

**Teaching:** Alignment over goosebumps. Month focus — Love vs Law — Understanding Grace: Obedience proves love; ritual cannot replace it. This week we carry "Forgiveness That Frees" (Ephesians 4:32). Mental Maturity & Clarity flows from being loved first. Let John 14:15 settle you: If you love me, keep my commands.

**Prayer:** Heavenly Father, we thank You for this brand-new day and the opportunity to gather as family in Your presence. Your mercies are new every morning. Forgive us our sins and trespasses, and we forgive all who trespassed against us. We stand cleansed by the blood of Jesus. Give me the mind of Christ. Cast down confusion and guard my thoughts with Your perfect peace.

**Declaration:** I have the mind of Christ; confusion has no hold on me.

**Decree:** I have the mind of Christ; confusion has no hold on me. If you love me, keep my commands. In Jesus’ name, Amen.

**Action:** Replace one anxious thought with the day verse — speak it out loud.
