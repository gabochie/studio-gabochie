Yearly message: Focus on The Love of The Lord. Focus: Love in action horizontally.

## Format 1 — Daily App / Audio (31 days)

### 2027-10-01 (Fri) — Wisdom for the Abundant Life [service-051 The End of the Matter]

**Scripture:** Mark 12:31 — Love your neighbour as yourself.

**Teaching:** Proximity is ministry. Month focus — Second Commandment — Loving Your Neighbour: Love in action horizontally. This week we carry "The End of the Matter" (Ecclesiastes 12:13). Wisdom for the Abundant Life flows from being loved first. Let Mark 12:31 settle you: Love your neighbour as yourself.

**Prayer:** Heavenly Father, we thank You for this brand-new day and the opportunity to gather as family in Your presence. Your mercies are new every morning. Forgive us our sins and trespasses, and we forgive all who trespassed against us. We stand cleansed by the blood of Jesus. Give me wisdom to steward resources well. Break scarcity; teach me generosity.

**Declaration:** I walk in Kingdom abundance to be a blessing.

**Decree:** I walk in Kingdom abundance to be a blessing. Love your neighbour as yourself. In Jesus’ name, Amen.

**Action:** Review one money choice; give or save as worship today.
