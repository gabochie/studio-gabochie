### 2027-11-01 (Mon) — Spiritual Strength & Growth [service-049 Thanksgiving and Praise]

**Scripture:** Matthew 5:44 — Love your enemies, pray for persecutors.

**Decree:** I am rooted and established in the eternal love of God. Love your enemies, pray for persecutors. In Jesus’ name, Amen.
