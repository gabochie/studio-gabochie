Questions
Do I need any experience?
None. These programs are designed for beginners and experienced practitioners alike. The concepts are accessible, the exercises make them stick.
Can I switch programs later?
Yes. You can enroll in both programs at any time. Each has its own dashboard and progress tracking.
What happens after I enroll?
You get immediate access to the free sample module and a private dashboard. A welcome email with your dashboard link is sent to your inbox. Upgrade anytime to unlock the full curriculum.
How do I get back to my dashboard?
Visit gideonabochie.org/dashboard/ and enter the email you used to enroll. We'll look up your dashboard automatically.
