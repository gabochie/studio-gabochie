Questions
Do I need any experience?
None. Every course starts from the basics and builds up. Whether you're a complete beginner or already have some experience, the practical exercises will push you forward.
Can I take multiple courses at once?
Yes. You can enroll in as many courses as you want. Each has its own dashboard and progress tracking so you can learn at your own pace.
What happens after I enroll?
You get immediate access to the free sample module and a private dashboard. A welcome email with your dashboard link is sent to your inbox. Paid courses let you upgrade anytime to unlock the full curriculum.
How do I get back to my dashboard?
Visit studio.gabochie.com/dashboard/ and log in with your email and access code. Your progress is saved automatically.
