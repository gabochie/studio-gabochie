Questions

Frequently Asked

Can I change or cancel my subscription?
Yes, anytime. Contact us at info@gideonabochie.com and we will handle the change or cancellation within 24 hours. No lock-in contracts.
Can I pay with mobile money?
Yes! Flutterwave supports mobile money (Momo) for Ghana, as well as international cards and bank transfers. Mobile money is available at checkout.
What if I prefer to make a one-time donation?
You can make a one-time donation through the donation page or via Ko-fi or Buy Me a Coffee (see the One-Time Support section below).
Is my payment secure?
Absolutely. Flutterwave is PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant — the same security standard banks use. Your card and payment details are encrypted end-to-end.
Can international supporters subscribe?
Yes. Flutterwave accepts international cards (Visa, Mastercard) and displays the equivalent in your currency at checkout. You can also use Ko-fi or Buy Me a Coffee below.
