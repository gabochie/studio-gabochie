FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a branding project take?
A Basic project takes 1-2 weeks. Standard takes 3-4 weeks. Premium brand projects typically take 6-8 weeks from kickoff to delivery.
Do you rebrand existing organizations?
Yes. We specialize in rebranding and brand refresh projects. The process includes a brand audit to understand what is working and what needs to change.
What if I do not like the design?
We include revision rounds in every tier. Our process ensures we align on direction before moving to final design, so surprises are rare.
