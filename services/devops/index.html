FAQ

Common Questions

What tools and platforms do you work with?
AWS, Google Cloud, Azure, and Cloudflare; Docker and Kubernetes; Terraform and Pulumi for infrastructure as code; GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, and other pipelines; and Prometheus, Grafana, and OpenTelemetry for observability. I adapt to your existing stack rather than forcing a rewrite.
Can you work with our existing team?
Yes. I embed with your engineers, pair on delivery, and document everything so your team can own the platform after handover. Optional training and knowledge-transfer sessions are available.
How do you price engagements?
Every engagement is custom-quoted based on scope, complexity, and urgency. Choose an ad-hoc task, a monthly retainer for ongoing support, or a full project engagement — I will propose the right model after a free discovery call.
Do you offer discounts for nonprofits and churches?
Yes, I especially enjoy working with mission-driven organizations. Discounted rates are available for registered nonprofits and churches.
