FAQ

Common Questions

Do you write in Twi or other Ghanaian languages?
Currently, we work primarily in English. For projects that include Ghanaian language elements or translation, we can collaborate with our network of language specialists.
How do you handle attribution and credits?
For ghostwriting, you receive full author credit. Our agreement specifies the terms of attribution, and we maintain strict confidentiality about our role.
What is your editing process?
We use a three-pass system: structural edit (big picture), line edit (paragraph and sentence level), and copy edit (grammar, style, consistency). Proofreading is the final pass before publication.
