Common Questions

How far in advance should we book?
We recommend booking at least 4-6 weeks in advance for in-person events and 2-3 weeks for virtual events. However, we accommodate last-minute requests when possible.
What topics do you speak on?
Our core topics include leadership and vision building, architectural and design thinking, systems thinking, creative excellence, nation building, education and youth empowerment, and faith and culture.
Do you travel outside Ghana?
Yes, we accept international speaking engagements. Travel and accommodation costs are additional for events outside Ghana. Virtual keynotes are available globally.
