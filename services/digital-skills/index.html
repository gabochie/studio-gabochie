FAQ

Common Questions

What group sizes can you accommodate?
We work best with groups of 10-30 participants. For larger groups, we can run multiple cohorts or train-the-trainer sessions.
What topics do you cover?
Our core offerings include web development (HTML, CSS, JavaScript), AI tools (ChatGPT, image generation), design software (Canva, Figma), digital marketing, and data literacy.
Do you offer certificates of completion?
Yes, participants in multi-week programs receive a certificate of completion. For shorter workshops, we provide a letter of participation upon request.
