How we work

Small on purpose, clear by default

A founder, a fleet of AI agents, and humans hired for the judgment machines do not have.

Async first

Work from any country. Decisions and updates are written down. We ask for a few hours of overlap with UTC each week, not a fixed desk.

Humans over the machine

Our agents draft, send receipts and analyse data. You own quality, money, relationships and care — the parts that need a person.

Paid trials, never free spec work

Every role starts with a four-week paid trial and a scoped deliverable. If it is not a fit on either side, we part well.

Simple stack

Cloudflare Pages, D1, Brevo, GitHub and WhatsApp. You do not need all of them — only the ones your role touches.

The honest bit

What we can and cannot offer

So you can decide before you apply.

What we can offer

  • Published rates — the band on each card is what we can pay today.
  • Flexible hours — agree outcomes, then fit the work around your life.
  • Real ownership — your name on the work, and a direct line to the founder.
  • Free courses — the whole learning library is open to you.
  • A mission you can see — students, books and ministry that are already live.

What we cannot offer yet

  • Employment or benefits — these are contracts and retainers, not salaried roles.
  • Equipment budget — bring your own laptop and connection.
  • A large team — it is one founder and a small agent fleet. You will work independently.
  • Unlimited hours — scope is agreed up front and paid trials are capped.
  • Guaranteed permanence — roles grow only when verified revenue does.
