Frequently Asked Questions
What does each level cost?
Community is free. Impact is sized to your budget — a scholarship seat costs less than a team lunch, and we build the cohort around what you can give. Strategic is scoped and priced per program. You will always have the number in writing before committing.
Is there a financial commitment to partner?
Partnership starts with shared vision, not a financial threshold. Community partnerships are free; higher rungs involve sponsorship or revenue sharing. We will discuss what fits your capacity.
How do referral rewards work?
When your community members enroll in paid courses or purchase resources using your unique referral code, you earn a percentage of the revenue. Details are customized per partnership agreement.
Can we customize course content for our context?
Yes. We work with partners to adapt curriculum — adding contextual examples, local case studies, and translated key terms. Customization scope grows with the partnership level.
Can our students learn guitar without owning instruments yet?
Yes — the Guitar School starts on concepts, ear training and practice tools that need no instrument, and we help schools and partners source instruments through our music-retail partners.
How long does it take to set up a partnership?
Most partnerships are active within 2-3 weeks from initial conversation. We keep the process lightweight — a discovery call, a simple agreement, and you are ready to start.
