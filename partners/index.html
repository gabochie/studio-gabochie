Frequently Asked Questions
Is there a financial commitment to partner?
Partnership starts with shared vision, not a financial threshold. We work with organizations at every level. Some partnerships involve revenue sharing on referrals; others are purely missional. We will discuss what fits your capacity.
How do referral rewards work?
When your community members enroll in paid programs or purchase resources using your unique referral code, you earn a percentage of the revenue. Details are customized per partnership agreement.
Can we customize course content for our context?
Yes. We work with partners to adapt curriculum — adding contextual examples, local case studies, and translated key terms. Customization scope depends on the partnership level.
How long does it take to set up a partnership?
Most partnerships are active within 2-3 weeks from initial conversation. We keep the process lightweight — a discovery call, a simple agreement, and you are ready to start.
